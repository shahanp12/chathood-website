THE CHATHOOD WORKSPACE

More than message consolidation. A complete communication operations layer.

Chathood is designed around real operational work: receive the message, understand the customer, route the conversation, automate the routine and give your team the right context to respond.

01 / INBOX

My Inbox

See active customer conversations in real time, with unread states, priorities and a central place to manage communication volume.

02 / CONVERSATIONS

Conversation workspace

Keep the full thread, internal notes, assignments, tags, status and team handoffs together.

03 / CONTACTS

Customer context

Bring profile details, contact information, tags and associated conversations into a lightweight CRM-style view.

04 / REPORTS

Operational analytics

Understand conversation traffic, agent performance, workload, CSAT, SLAs, labels and bot insights.

05 / CAMPAIGNS

Proactive messaging

Use WhatsApp, SMS and live-chat campaigns for announcements, reminders, onboarding and re-engagement.

06 / HELP CENTER

Self-service

Publish FAQs, troubleshooting guides, documentation and onboarding content to reduce support load.
